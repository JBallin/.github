# Code of Conduct

We want this community to be welcoming and respectful. Everyone is welcome,
regardless of background, identity, or experience.

## How we work together

- Be kind, considerate, and patient. Respect other people's time and boundaries.
- Welcome different perspectives and give constructive feedback. Discuss ideas
  without attacking people.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and be willing to listen, learn, and make
  things right.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or make unwanted sexual comments or
  advances.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal or confidential
  information without permission.
- Be honest about who you are and give others credit for their work.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and events, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.
Disagreement by itself is not a violation.
